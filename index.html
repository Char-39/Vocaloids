Vocaloids

What is a Vocaloid?

Vocaloid is a singing voice synthesizer software. The processing part was developed by Yamaha Corporation and the Music Technology Group at Pompeu Fabra University, and was developed into a commercial product and released in 2004. The software lets you synthesize singing by typing in lyrics, melody, and speech as well as changing pitch, tone, pronunciation and other effects.

Each Vocaloid license develops the singer library, or a database of vocals sampled from real people, the database must have all possible combinations of phonemes of the target language. Though originally developed in the Japanese language there are also Vocaloid voice banks for English, Spanish, Chinese, Korean.

Other Vocaloids

Other Singing synthesizer

What are other types of Singing synthesizer?

An UTAloid is a free virtual singer that is user-created, meaning the voicebank is made by users who record their own voice. The difference from a Vocaloid is that it's not commercially made. par1

Another type of virtual singer is a Fanloid. A Fanloid is a character inspired and or based off of an officially made Vocaloid, created by fans and the community. They Typically are derivative characters like genderbends, character variants or mascots that don't have official voicebanks, with fans creating their voices by modifying, pitching, shifting, or tuning existing Vocaloids. (I would also like to mention that the popular streamer Caseoh has been made into a Fanloid using his own voice.)

Kasane Teto

The first UTAloid created was Teto, a female Utaloid that has gained a large popularity as of 2024 and is now one of the most recognizable virtual singer. Voiced by Oyamano Mayo, Teto was originally created as an April fools joke in 2008 but was later made into an actual voicebank later that year. Some of her most notable and popular songs include "Spoken For" by FLAVOR FOLEY - "BIRDBRAIN" by Jamie Paige - "Love Machine" by Jamie Paige- "Language Of The Lost" by RIProducer - "Mezmerizer" by Satsuki - "Teto The 31st" by Hallo Cel - "Medicine" by Sasuke Haraguchi - "Lair Macaron" by GluttonousP - "Tetoris" by Hiiragi Magnetite - "PPPP" by TAK - "HITO Mania" by Sasuke Haraguchi - "CandyCookieChocolate" by Hallo Cel - "Execution Clap" by TRAP CHICK - "Override" by Yoshida Yasei - "Ochame Kinou (Mischievous Function)" by LamazaP - "Daidaidaidaidaikirai" by Amalaa and "Triple Baka" by LamazeP. Over 1600 songs use Teto's voice. Teto is often associated with a baguette. Her surname 重音 (Kasane) literally means "heavy sound" or "overlapped sound" while her name テト (Teto) comes from Tetopettenson, a parody song of Le Beau Tambour.

Adachi Rei

Adachi Rei is a female UTAloid with a completely AI voicebank. Her voice was artificially generated in Audacity— a free and open-source digital audio editor and recording application software— specifically to create a robotic and choppy sounding voice. There are approximately 146 songs with her voice some being "Heat Abnormal" by Iyowa - "Dance Delightful" by Jamie Paige - "Queen Of Venus" by FLAVOR FOLEY - "My Bread Was Burnt To A Crisp" by Picdo - "ONE" by Staircatte - "HIROIN RE♡Quest" by Samsap - "GIMMICK GIRL" by Phantoskii - "Super Complex" by Shimon. Rei can sometimes be associated with fried chicken due to fan-driven memes. Her name combines "Adachi"(足立/安達), it may imply "to stand on one's feet" (足立), while "Rei" (レイ) relates to "0" (Prototype 0).

Akita Neru

Akita Neru is a Fanloid derived from Hatsune Miku, created by Smith Hioka. She has not been given a definitive voice, with some producers giving her a boyish-sounding girl voice as a lower pitched Miku voice, while some have given her a higher pitched Miku voice. Another suggestion also has her using Kagamine Rin's voice. She doesn't have a precise, official song count, but there are many user-submitted covers and fan-made content, but there are at least 66 songs on Spotify.A few of her songs are "FLOP ERA" by ePiaeon - "Zako" by Hiiragi Magnetite - "Baumkuchen End Credits" by Amalaa - "Gocha Gocha Urusee! (Stop Nagging Me!/ Shut Up With Your Complains!)" by Owate-P - "Triple Baka" by Lamaza-P - "Chimera Theater" by SoChromatic and "Boxed-in Utopia" by Kousaku-P -. Neru is often associated with a flip phone due to her appearance with it in the song "Triple Baka". Her name is a play on words: Akita (亞北) is taken from "飽きた" or "get bored" while Neru (ネル) is from "寝る" or "go to bed".

Calne Ca

Calne Ca is another Fanloid derived from Miku, Created by Deino. She has a creepy robotic bug-like appearance with a robotic eye, a missing jaw with robotic spider looking legs coming out, as well as elongated legs, a long robotic arm with a claw-like hand,and a skeleton arm and hand on the other side. The main songs associated with her are "Saikin Osen (Bacterial Contamination)" by Mathru and "Machine Muzik" by Deino, but also sings "Mukuro Attack!! (Corpse Attack!!)" by Utsu-P. Her name comes from kara (骸, "corpse") and ne (音, "sound"), as well as Ca (シーエ), the chemical symbol of calcium. This is due to Calne Ca being a mechanized skeleton with the appearance of Miku.
